# Travel and Expense Policy

Business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before booking.
Travel should be booked through the approved corporate portal. Economy class
is required for flights under six hours; premium economy may be used for
longer flights with director approval.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days and include itemized receipts
for expenses above $25. Meals are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Approved
claims are normally paid in the next payroll cycle.
