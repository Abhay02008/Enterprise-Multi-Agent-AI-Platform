# Work From Home Policy

Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to three days per week with
manager approval. Teams define common in-office collaboration days. Employees
must remain available during their local core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 and use
company-managed devices and the corporate VPN.

Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department director and
HR. The company may ask employees to attend important in-person meetings with
reasonable notice.
