# Leave Policy

Employees receive 12 casual leave days and 12 sick leave days each calendar
year. Casual leave is credited monthly and unused casual leave expires at the
end of the year. Employees should request planned leave at least three working
days in advance through the HR portal.

Eligible employees may take 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two
children. Requests require a medical certificate and should be submitted to HR
at least eight weeks before the expected start date. An additional four weeks
of unpaid leave may be requested with manager and HR approval.
